Audio tuning

Thresholds and band edges tune the beat detector and scene classifier. Values update live as you drag. Use Calibrate 30s on the main panel to auto-set most of them from observed music.

AutoLight training

Pick a folder of audio files (or individual tracks) for the AI to learn from. While "Training" is on, drag the satisfaction slider in real time as the rig reacts — the AI saves your feedback per track and uses it on later listens. Playback can come from the app (via "Play" buttons) or from any external player; the AI listens through the system audio either way.
